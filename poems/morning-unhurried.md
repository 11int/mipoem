---
title: Morning, Unhurried
author: M. Poem
date: 2026-05-12
tags: [morning, calm, light]
---

The kettle hums a small blue song,
the window holds a square of fog.
I am not late for anything yet —
the day is still a folded note.

Steam writes a sentence on the glass
and wipes it clean before I read.
Somewhere a sparrow tries the air
like a word it hasn't said before.
