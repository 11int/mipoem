---
title: Letter to a Houseplant
author: M. Poem
date: 2025-12-21
tags: [home, growth, small things]
---

You ask for so little — a window,
a thimble of rain on Sundays,
the occasional rotation toward light.
And still you green, you reach, you mean it.

Teach me your slow ambition,
your way of leaning without leaving,
how to grow toward what warms me
and call that a whole life's work.
