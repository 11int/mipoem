---
title: Instructions for a Slow Evening
author: Lena Cole
date: 2026-01-30
tags: [evening, rest, ritual]
---

Turn the lamp to its lowest honesty.
Let the radio find a station of rain.
Pour something warm you don't have to finish.
Forgive the dishes their patient crowd.

Sit until the room forgets you're there,
until the chair believes it grew you.
This is not laziness. This is a vigil
for the small animal of your attention.
