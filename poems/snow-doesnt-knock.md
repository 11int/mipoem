---
title: Snow Doesn't Knock
author: Idris Vale
date: 2025-11-14
tags: [winter, snow, quiet]
---

Snow doesn't knock. It simply arrives,
the way forgiveness sometimes does,
covering the argument of the yard
until the fence forgets its line.

By morning everything is rounded,
the sharp world wearing mittens.
Even my footprints, going nowhere,
look like a sentence kindly meant.
