---
title: Inventory of a Pocket
author: M. Poem
date: 2026-03-08
tags: [small things, memory]
---

One ticket stub, the film forgotten.
A button that belongs to no shirt.
Lint, the gray weather of pockets.
A receipt for bread and a apology.

These are the things I carry quietly,
the archive no museum wants —
proof that I was somewhere, briefly,
and paid for it in small change.
