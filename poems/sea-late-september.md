---
title: Sea, Late September
author: Idris Vale
date: 2026-02-17
tags: [sea, autumn, longing]
---

The sea keeps practising one word
and never gets the ending right.
It tries, withdraws, and tries again,
patient as grief, as tides, as light.

I came to give the water something —
a name, a stone, a folded year.
It took the stone without a thank-you
and left the name still standing here.
