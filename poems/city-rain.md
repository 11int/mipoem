---
title: City Rain
author: Lena Cole
date: 2026-04-29
tags: [city, rain, night]
---

The streetlights bleed into the wet,
each one a coin dropped in a well.
Umbrellas argue at the crossing,
then settle, black and democratic.

I love the way the city softens —
how every edge agrees to blur,
how strangers share a single awning
and, for a minute, share a weather.
