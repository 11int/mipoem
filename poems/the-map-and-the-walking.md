---
title: The Map and the Walking
author: Lena Cole
date: 2025-10-02
tags: [travel, time, hope]
---

A map is a promise drawn in patience —
*here* is a river, *there* a town,
and all the white between them folded
into the pocket of an afternoon.

But maps are quiet about the walking,
the blister, the wrong and lovely turn,
the dog that joins you for a mile
and the field you'll never name but keep.
